## What's DecentHousing?

DecentHousing is a highly advanced creative game mode for Minecraft servers which allows players to create their own plots on floating islands, and publish them for the public to visit.

## Why DecentHousing for Your Server?

/// html | div.grid.cards

- :material-account-group: **Boost Player Engagement**

    Housing is a community-driven game mode. Your players will always have a new content to explore with friends and other players. They create it themselves. :)

- :material-cash-multiple: **Monetization**

    Easily sell or grant custom plot packages, extra plot limits, unlockable items, special tags, and rank perks in your webstore or rank rewards.

- :material-tune-variant: **100% Configurable (No Coding Required)**

    Configure everything effortlessly through clean YML files and in-game GUI menus. Adjust messages, prices, items, permissions, and plot limits without writing a single line of code.

- :material-lightning-bolt: **Lag-Free High Performance**

    Built from the ground up to handle high player counts and many, many active plots seamlessly without slowing down your main game servers.
